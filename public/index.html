Process

Direct collaboration, short feedback loops and a deliberately small surface area.

From problem to working software without the theatre.

The goal is not to produce a large specification. It is to understand the workflow well enough to build the smallest version that genuinely improves it, then ship it properly.

  1. 01 / scope

    Understand the workflow

    Map what happens today, who touches it, where time disappears and what success would actually look like.

    Clear scope and product direction
  2. 02 / build

    Build the real thing

    Implement around the actual use case, validate the interaction early and keep unnecessary complexity out.

    A working application, not a slide deck
  3. 03 / ship

    Deploy and operate it

    Production setup, reliability and maintainability are treated as part of the product from the beginning.

    Software ready to be used and evolved
