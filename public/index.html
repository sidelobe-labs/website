On demand

Software shaped around the workflow — not the other way around.

When an off-the-shelf tool almost works, the remaining gap is often where the real friction lives. Sidelobe builds a focused application around that gap: small enough to stay clear, specific enough to be genuinely useful.

Internal tools Replace spreadsheet-heavy, email-heavy or manual processes with one purpose-built interface.
Workflow applications Planning, approvals, scheduling, coordination and operations shaped around the way your team works.
Prototypes Turn a product idea into something concrete enough to put in front of real users and learn from.
Automation Connect existing systems and remove repetitive work when a little custom logic goes a long way.
A typical starting point is simply: “we do this in a spreadsheet, inbox or chat today, and it is becoming painful.”
Process

From an operational problem to a shipped tool, without unnecessary ceremony.

01 / scope

Understand the workflow

Map what happens today, who touches it, where time is lost and what the smallest useful version should solve.

Outcome: clear scope and product direction
02 / build

Make the real thing

Build around the actual use case, validate the interaction early and keep the implementation intentionally focused.

Outcome: working application, not a slide deck
03 / ship

Deploy and operate it

Production setup, observability and maintainability are treated as part of the product rather than an afterthought.

Outcome: a tool that is ready to be used
